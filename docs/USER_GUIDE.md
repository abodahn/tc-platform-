# TC Platform — User Guide

Welcome to **TC Platform**, T&C Garments' unified digital operations portal.

## Signing in
Open the platform URL (e.g. `http://10.100.1.13`). Enter your username and
password. Forgot it? Contact IT / an administrator.

## The top bar
- **☰ Menu** — collapse/expand the sidebar (state is remembered).
- **Search** — find apps and modules by name or category.
- **EN / ع / TR** — switch language. Arabic flips the layout to right-to-left.
- **☀ / ☾ / 🖥** — Light, Dark or Auto (follow your OS) theme.
- **🔔 Bell** — notifications (SLA breaches, offline servers, low stock, …).
  Use *Mark all read* to clear the dot.
- **Your name** — profile and **Sign out**.

Your language and theme are saved to your profile and follow you on any device.

## Command Center (home)
Your executive overview:
- KPI tiles (tickets, SLA, assets, servers, projects, approvals, savings…).
- **Digital Transformation Progress** bars and an **SLA Health** ring.
- **System Health** cards — green *Online* / red *Offline* for each app.
- **Today's Focus** and **Critical Attention Required**.

## App Launcher
Cards for every system. Integrated apps show a live status badge and an **Open**
button (new tab by default). **Open module** shows details and launch options.
Future-ready modules (AI, Finance, Automation, BI, Production, HR, Procurement…)
open structured pages that preview what's coming.

## Opening an existing system (inside the platform)
Click a system in the **sidebar** or its **Open** button in the Launcher — the
**full app loads right inside TC Platform**: the platform sidebar and topbar stay
in place and the app appears in the main area. A slim bar above it lets you:
- **Refresh** the app, **Full screen** (open it in its own tab), or view **Details**.

You still sign in to each app the first time (unified single sign-on arrives in a
later phase). If a system is offline, you'll see a retry panel instead of a frame.

> Tip: use **Full screen** if you want the app to fill the whole browser window.

## Reports, Health, Roadmap
- **Reports** — executive and operational reports (exports coming soon).
- **System Health** — technical status of the platform and all integrations.
- **Roadmap** — what's live now and what's planned next.

## Tips
- If an app shows **Offline**, it isn't running or its address changed — tell IT.
- Numbers animate on load; a hard refresh (Ctrl+F5) reloads everything.
- On mobile, tap ☰ to open the menu; cards stack into a single column.
