How the step works

  • Only the person picked here (or someone they delegated to) can sign the step — no role or admin can, except the super admin in place.
  • The requester never signs their own request: if the controller raises it, the step is skipped and the request says so.
  • One person cannot sign both Item control and Warehouse Manager on the same request.
  • Changing the person moves the requests already waiting at this step to the new person. Saving a person also adds the step to this group's open requests; one already past the Warehouse Manager waits for the item controller before it continues.
  • A request with a CHM item (code like 00-CHM-0001) is also signed by the Chemicals & Stones controller, before the Warehouse Manager. If its department has its own controller, both sign — the department's first.
